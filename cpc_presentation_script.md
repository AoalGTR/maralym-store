# Smart Student Calculator & Budget App

## Full Presentation Script (10 Slides)

### Slide 1: Title

**Thank you for joining us today.**

Good day, my name is [your name].
Today I am presenting Smart Student Calculator and Budget App,
an all-in-one calculator and money organizer for students.

**In this presentation, I am going to walk you through the problem,**
our solution, business model, and growth plan.

Students calculate every day and spend every day, but these actions happen in separate apps.
Our idea is to combine both in one simple workflow.

### Slide 2: Problem

**Does everyone have a copy of the report we'll discuss today?**
**Can you see the presentation I'm sharing on my screen?**

**Let's dive into the first part of our presentation.**

Students usually use multiple tools:
1. Calculator for class and daily tasks.
2. Notes or chat for recording expenses.
3. Another app for budget tracking.

This creates friction, missed entries, and weak financial habits.
At the end of the month, many students cannot clearly explain where their money went.

### Slide 3: Solution

Our solution is one app with four core functions:
1. Basic and scientific calculator.
2. Fast expense tracking by category.
3. Monthly budget goals with overspending alerts.
4. Simple analytics dashboard.

The key value is speed: calculate, save, and track in one place.

### Slide 4: Target Market

Our primary segment is students aged 17 to 25.
Our secondary segment is young professionals.

We launch campus-first through university communities,
then scale regionally based on retention and referral metrics.

### Slide 5: Business Model

Our model is freemium:
1. Free tier for core usage and onboarding.
2. Premium at $2.99 per month for advanced insights and cloud sync.
3. Campus partnerships and student bundles.
4. Optional ad-supported free tier.

This combination balances growth, affordability, and recurring revenue.

### Slide 6: Financial Plan

Year-1 estimates:
1. Startup costs: $6,000.
2. Monthly operating cost: about $1,200.
3. Active user goal: 10,000.
4. Premium conversion target: 5 to 8 percent.
5. Break-even target: month 10 to 12.

The financial model is designed for controlled growth and early sustainability.

### Slide 7: Go-To-Market

Growth channels:
1. Campus ambassadors and referral rewards.
2. Social media campaigns in student communities.
3. University demos and student club presentations.
4. App Store optimization and review strategy.

This gives us both direct acquisition and peer-to-peer organic growth.

### Slide 8: Competition and Advantage

Most competitors are either calculator-only or finance-only.

Our advantage:
1. One integrated flow for calculation and budgeting.
2. Simpler experience than heavy finance apps.
3. Lower price point for students.
4. Daily utility that supports higher retention.

### Slide 9: 12-Month Roadmap

Q1: MVP launch and onboarding.
Q2: 1,000 users and premium release.
Q3: Partnerships and retention improvements.
Q4: Scale to 10,000 users and approach break-even.

The roadmap is realistic, measurable, and execution-focused.

### Slide 10: Funding Ask and Closing

We are seeking $25,000 in pre-seed funding.

Use of funds:
1. 45 percent product development.
2. 35 percent marketing and growth.
3. 15 percent operations.
4. 5 percent legal and admin.

Smart Student Calculator is not just a feature set.
It is a practical daily habit for students and a scalable business opportunity.

## Short 30-Second Pitch

Smart Student Calculator combines a calculator and personal budget tracker in one app.
It solves the common student problem of switching between multiple tools for daily calculations and spending control.
With a freemium model and affordable premium plan, we target strong adoption, stable retention, and scalable growth through campus channels.
