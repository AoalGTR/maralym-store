Problem

Students jump between multiple apps for math and money tracking. This causes poor budgeting consistency and low retention.

Solution

  • Calculator: basic + scientific modes
  • Expense tracking by category
  • Monthly budget goals and overspending alerts
  • Simple weekly and monthly analytics

Target Market

  • Primary: college students (17-25)
  • Secondary: young professionals
  • Launch: campus-first, then regional scale

Go-To-Market

  • Campus ambassador program with referral rewards
  • Micro-campaigns in Telegram, TikTok, and Instagram
  • Student club demo sessions every semester
  • App Store optimization with student-focused keywords

Competitive Advantage

  • Single app for both calculations and budgeting
  • Faster daily workflow than split-tool competitors
  • Low-price premium model for student affordability
  • High retention potential through daily calculator usage
